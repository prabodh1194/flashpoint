01 / the problemA SaaS data platform is a third-party risk.

Every hosted warehouse adds a data processor to your register, an egress path to review, and a residency question to answer. For many teams the review alone costs more than the platform.

02 / what changesSame facts, better surface

Today

  • A processor to assess and contract
  • An egress and residency review
  • Credentials to a system you do not own

With Flashpoint

  • Runs on your IAM and your S3
  • No data egress by design
  • MIT licensed, auditable source

03 / how it worksThree steps

Deploy into your account

OpenTofu brings up the gateway, the task definitions and the state store in your AWS account.

Keep your perimeter

Compute runs in your VPC. Results land in your bucket. Your IAM decides who can reach what.

Audit the source

The control plane is MIT licensed, so your reviewers read the code instead of a whitepaper.

04 / questionsStraight answers

Do you see our data?

No. There is no hosted service in the path. The gateway, the compute and the results all run in your account.

Where does state live?

In your DynamoDB, in your account. The gateway is stateless and rebuilds from that state.

Can we run it without public access?

The deployment can sit in private subnets. Reaching the endpoint is your networking choice, not a requirement of the product.

Is the source auditable?

Yes. The control plane is MIT licensed and the deployment is plain OpenTofu, so reviewers can read every line.
